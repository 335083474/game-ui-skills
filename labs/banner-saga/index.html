Caravan preparation ↗

A hand-painted snow-coast plate frames three unequal road-party tokens and a stitched provisions ledger. Choosing a speaker previews the consequence; a separate confirmation records it.

ROADSIDE-CHOICE

Roadside event ↗

A short broken-ferry vignette places two grounded choices beside the current food and fatigue counts. Costs and outcomes appear before the player confirms.

CAMP-TRADE

Camp trade ↗

A low-noise barter ledger shows dried-fish stock, condition, price, and remaining food. One purchase can be explicitly confirmed or cancelled.
