# Visual rules

## Cartographic identity

Make the strategic map the dominant spatial object. Use discrete hex terrain tiles with small illustrated features, fine contour lines, and soft atmospheric gaps. The atlas provides original terrain tiles and civic symbols. Avoid an unstructured painted background; tile boundaries and hex adjacency must remain visible.

The control surface uses deep navy, muted blue, and restrained antique gold. Use a warm ivory paper only for small map labels or pinned notes. This is a formal map table with clear state, not a hand-drawn potion map. No diagonal ink route or torn trading sticker should appear.

## Controls and typography

Use a compact sans for controls and numbers, with a moderate serif for major era or civic headings. Resource readouts use small circular or shield-shaped medallions and fixed columns. Keep icons consistent in size and pair every icon with its label or accessible name. Gold marks current selection, actionable research, or a confirmed offer; it is never a full-panel background.

## Research, production, diplomacy

Research nodes occupy a branching prerequisite graph. Connectors end at node centers; completed, available, and blocked states have different shape and label, not just color. Costs and turns remain visible beside or within the node. City production uses a numbered vertical queue with a movable reorder handle, turn estimate, and current item preview; changing order visibly recalculates positions. Diplomatic trade uses two clear offer columns and a compact equalizer/balance line. Show resource quantity and agreement status explicitly.

Keep secondary info in a narrow overlay or selected-node panel. Do not obscure the map with three rows of equivalent large cards. Avoid fantasy runes, ornate corner scrollwork, thick bevels, and glossy gold buttons.

## Accessibility and responsive

Use keyboard traversal for map tiles and graph nodes, with a separate textual selection summary. Focus uses a 2px gold outline plus a label such as “Selected tile.” Arrow keys move across adjacent hexes; Escape closes the selected detail pane. At narrow widths, preserve map aspect ratio with horizontal pan and place the detail panel below the map. Respect reduced motion; animate only a committed queue reorder or trade confirmation.
