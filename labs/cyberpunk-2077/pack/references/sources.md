- `assets/art/scene.svg`, `assets/art/icons.svg`, and `references/images/style-reference.svg` are original local vector files made for this package. They contain no source-game screenshots, extracted textures, official logos, or character art.

## Limits

- All vector art, implant names, district labels, and sample values are original; no Cyberpunk 2077 game assets or font are included.

- No engine integration, gameplay logic, or measured search-demand evidence is included.
- Search volume, ranking, and product demand are unknown; this package makes no demand claim.
