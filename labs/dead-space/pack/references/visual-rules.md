# Visual rules

## Projection grammar

- Use a near-black graphite field with engineered edges, mounting ticks, alignment marks, and clipped corners.
- Cyan is the information and tracking light. Use it for fine rules, selected outlines, and path geometry. Keep large surfaces dark and opaque enough for text.
- Amber is a small caution or cost signal. State the caution in words; do not use color alone.
- Every projection has a purpose: suit equipment, a kiosk transaction, or a route vector.
- Create spatial feel with a few offset frames, perspective rails, or alignment crosses. Keep text horizontal and undistorted.
- Keep glow tight and faint. Avoid large blurred neon, full-panel scanlines, and glass effects beneath dense text.

## Screen differences

RIG Inventory compares installed equipment, one pending module, and suit resources. Use slot positions and a selected-module inspection pane. Confirm changes one module; cancel restores the installed part.

Store Kiosk is a transaction surface. Show balance, item description, price, and remaining balance before commit. Disable purchases above available demo credit. Confirm and cancel have explicit feedback.

Locator is spatial guidance. Show one tracked objective, bearing vector, distance, and visible landmarks. Do not reuse the inventory grid or turn the path into unrelated cards.

## Readability and input

- Put text on a solid dark field. Decorative lines never cross small labels.
- Pair state with a word or number and shape, not cyan or amber alone.
- Use native buttons with clear focus. Preview before commit and provide cancel.
- On mobile, keep one projection surface in view and stack controls below it.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion. No status depends on flicker, pulse, or motion.

## Boundaries

EA’s official product page supports the 2023 remake scope and shows holo-display scenes. These rules interpret that material for an original browser specimen. The package does not verify exact UI layouts, suit projection mechanics, resource values, locator behavior, or engine integration.
