# Delivery checks

- Open the built result offline. All imagery, fonts, styles and route resources resolve locally. No dependency on the original absolute workspace path or CDN.
- Compare against both reference image and editable board: weighty dark rectangular frame, thin old-gold headings, small crisp text, equipment silhouette anatomy, multi-cell backpack, opposing life/mana globes. No oversized retail cards or modern rounded dashboard treatment.
- Count the backpack: exactly 10 × 4. Verify every rectangle stays in bounds and no two overlap; occupied-cell count must account for the full item footprint. One sprite per item, no cropping bleed from adjacent atlas cells.
- Exercise equip, compatible replacement, incompatible slot, missing requirement, full-bag swap refusal and unequip. Confirm item-ID conservation and atomic failure. Derived stats must return to baseline when unequipped.
- Exercise a skill prerequisite failure, level/rank cap, successful one-point spend, zero unspent points and tab change. Verify spent ranks plus remaining points reconcile with the initial budget. Item bonuses never unlock prerequisite ranks.
- Buy once, buy to sold-out, attempt purchase without gold, attempt purchase without room, and sell once. Check gold delta, stock delta, unique instance creation/removal, and no change on rejected transactions. Navigation preserves the same bag and gold.
- Keyboard through tabs, item selection, action and close. Every action has a visible focus treatment, descriptive accessible name and feedback. Locked explanations are available without hovering. At narrow width, no unreachable clipped cells or controls.
- Distinguish generated reference, editable component specimen and new finished screen in handoff. Cite official source links for classic claims and label sample mechanics/art. A successful build is not proof that all interaction states were checked.
