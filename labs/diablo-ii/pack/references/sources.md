# Primary-source research and interpretation

Checked 2026-10-16. This package targets the original Diablo II bitmap-menu direction, not Diablo III, IV or Resurrected's remastered renderer. It supplies original generated studies, not official screenshots.

## Official manual

[Blizzard Diablo II manual](https://download.blizzard.com/pub/misc/Diablo%20II%20Manual.pdf) · [official FTP-host alternative](https://ftp.blizzard.com/pub/misc/Diablo%20II%20Manual.pdf).

The 48-page PDF contains paired printed pages. Direct browser fetch timed out; local retrieval from the requested official download URL succeeded after bypassing a certificate-name mismatch for this public PDF only. The document was inspected locally and is not redistributed in this package.

- PDF page 9 / printed page 17: red life orb lower left, blue mana orb lower right, interface controls below the play area.
- PDF page 11 / printed pages 20–21: body-location equipment wells, lower backpack with variable-size items, item descriptions, pickup/drop/swap and equip behavior. The inventory occupies half the play area.
- PDF page 13 / printed page 25: merchant stock left, player items right, category tabs, price/property descriptions and gold deductions.
- PDF page 16 / printed page 30: three skill categories, branching prerequisites, available point count, skill descriptions and next-rank information.

## Official supplementary guide

[The Arreat Summit: Skill Basics](https://classic.battle.net/diablo2exp/skills/basics.shtml) verifies hard-point prerequisites, rank cap 20 and a per-character-level rank limit. Item-granted skills do not satisfy prerequisites. This is Blizzard's expansion-era guide and includes later-patch topics; do not import its respec, synergy, expansion-class or item systems into a purported original-release recreation without specifying the version.

[Blizzard's current Diablo II page](https://diablo2.blizzard.com/en-us/) is a product/edition check, not the visual authority for this original-menu package. Direct content retrieval was unavailable during this pass.

## Authored interpretation

The palette, substitute OFL fonts, CSS bevel/stone treatment, item art, responsive stage, sample data and finite-stock trade contract are authored decisions. The generated direction image has enlarged decorative framing and is a visual study, not exact game geometry. The editable board corrects structural requirements such as the 10 × 4 backpack. The sample shop uses carried gold only; original vendor payment can include stash gold. Explicit selection plus action buttons and keyboard equivalents adapt the pointer-led original for usable Web experiments.

No Blizzard logos, extracted textures, official item sprites, official font, official screenshot or full manual are bundled. Generated art is logged in `generation.json`; it should not be presented as proof of official game mechanics or production licensing.
