# Visual grammar

## Structure

Use a heavy rectangular carved frame around a dense functional surface. Charcoal stone, tarnished cold iron and worn edge highlights should feel weight-bearing. Recessed black cells and thin low-contrast separators hold the content. Square corners and 2–5 px layered bevels replace rounded containers. Keep close groupings: 8–16 px within a panel, 16–24 px between systems. Frames are more prominent than dividers; do not give every row an independent card.

Classic menus overlap or split the game view. A standalone Web experiment may place the active menu in a centered 1100–1280 px stage with a dark surround; label this as a responsive interpretation. On narrow viewports scroll or stack functional panes while preserving their internal grid. Do not shrink text below readability or clip inventory cells. A mobile bottom-tab application is a different visual direction.

## Color, type, ornament

Use near-black warm gray for recesses, cool graphite for stone, and desaturated bronze for bevel highlights. Old gold is a restrained title/selection accent, not a page-wide highlight. Life is deep red and mana is cobalt blue; these saturated globe colors carry the strongest accents. Resource globes belong near opposite bottom corners of a stage, with number labels and a small central belt/action strip.

Alegreya SC supplies small-cap serif headings; Crimson Text supplies compact readable serif text. These OFL substitutes are not Diablo's proprietary font. Prefer 20–26 px headings, 16–18 px UI text, 14–15 px secondary labels, 12 px minimum for incidental slot hints. Keep statistics aligned and numbers tabular. A shadow may separate pale type from stone; avoid blurry text and all-caps paragraphs.

Use sparse spikes, joints, double lines, inset corners and small diamond marks. Decorative gothic relief may occupy a frame or side rail, never cover text, slots or focus states. CSS stone texture is deliberately subtle and editable. Avoid purple neon, yellow-filled CTA pills, glass dashboard cards, excessive drop shadows, huge headings, sans-serif metrics and photographic character heroes.

## Item and skill anatomy

Equipment slots form a body: head high, amulet beside neck, weapon and shield at either side of torso, gloves/belt/boots lower, two small ring wells. Empty wells need subdued silhouette cues and text alternatives. Do not replace this with a list of equipment cards.

Backpack is 10 columns × 4 rows. One item owns one rectangle of cells; show one painted icon centered in that rectangle. Item hover/selection gets a fine pale/gold edge. Invalid equipment uses a restrained dark-red wash and explicit reason. Ordinary item names are pale; magic blue, rare yellow, unique gold are optional semantic name treatments, not rarity-colored whole panels.

Skill trees use three compact category tabs, square engraved icons, rank fractions and visible prerequisite connectors. Darken locked icons without making their lock reason disappear. A finite count of available points belongs beside the title; spending visibly changes the count and rank.

Merchant panes share inventory cell geometry and tooltips. Display numeric price and carried gold near the action. Keep Buy/Sell/Close as small iron buttons. Selected item details may use an inset description area; do not make a retail product grid.

## Interaction rendering

Represent default, hover, pressed, keyboard focus, selected, disabled and invalid. Hover raises a 1 px warm edge; pressed reverses bevel. Focus uses a thin cream outline outside the border. Disabled controls retain readable labels with an adjacent reason; selection uses a corner mark or border as well as color. Tooltips use an opaque dark field, pale body, compact colored name and a narrow border. Support focus/click access to their content, not hover only.
