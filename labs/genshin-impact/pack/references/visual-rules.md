# Visual rules

## Shape and material

Use a pale cream surface, quiet navy, and a thin gold line. Build frames from fine double rules, star points, feather cuts, and open corner ornaments. Let the character silhouette and sky remain visible; avoid heavy opaque panels and crowded borders.

## Type and emphasis

Use a local book serif for names and a restrained sans for stats. A single navy fill may anchor a selected action or item. Keep gold for thin trim, small markers, and stars. Label equipment and quest states in text so they remain readable without color.

## Screen grammar

Character selection pairs an original traveler illustration with a short profile and three attributes. Equipment emphasizes one clear slot and the item's level or effect. The quest journal uses a slim, readable list with current objective separated from optional notes.

## Adaptation

Use [screen-patterns.md](screen-patterns.md) for character, equipment, and quest-journal layouts. This package targets an early Mondstadt-era visual direction and makes no claim about the current live build. All character art, names, and sample stats are original.
