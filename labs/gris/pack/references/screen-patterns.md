# Screen patterns and synthetic behavior

## Chapter selection (chapter-selection)

Arrange a few named chapters along a sparse path, arch, or stepped ledge. Mark visited, current, and not-yet-reached chapters with shape and readable text. Selection previews the destination only; Enter chapter is the explicit transition.

## Memories (memories)

Show a small fragment with a found or unknown state, short title, and an open/close reading action. Do not create an invented collectible economy or claim these records are canonical GRIS collectibles. Keep the detail text on a quiet opaque field so watercolor texture does not reduce legibility.

## Pause (pause)

Offer Continue first, then at most a few secondary destinations such as Chapters or Controls. Keep the underlying scene visible. Do not expand a full preferences form on the initial pause surface.

## Synthetic contract

demo-state.json invents three chapter labels and one memory record. Use stable IDs if adapting the structure. Selecting a chapter sets previewChapter but does not change currentChapter. Opening a found memory changes only visibleMemory. Any persistent collection update requires real product data and an explicit action defined by the target game.

## Observable behavior

- Chapter buttons expose pressed state and a visible current/preview label.
- Enter chapter is separate from selecting a chapter.
- Memory content opens and closes with a readable live-status update.
- Continue is explicit and has no hidden side effect.
- Keyboard order is predictable, focus remains visible, and reduced motion removes decorative movement.
