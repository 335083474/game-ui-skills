# Visual rules

## Shape and material

Build the surface from charcoal stone, pomegranate red, and warm bronze. Use angular frames, a Greek-key rhythm, laurel cuts, and small gem-like marks as recurring geometry. Keep bronze edges narrow; a screen should feel carved and lit from within, not covered in gold.

## Type and emphasis

Use a high-contrast local serif for mythic names and a compact sans or monospace face for run values. Keep headings short. Reserve the warm red for an action or dangerous state; use bronze for selection and rarity. Pair every colored state with a word, shape, or icon.

## Choice anatomy

An offering card pairs an original divine or weapon emblem with a short title, a small category line, and one gameplay effect. Keep the three choices visually equal until one receives a double bronze focus edge. Separate the confirm action from the item descriptions.

## Adaptation

Use [screen-patterns.md](screen-patterns.md) to adjust the same grammar for boons, weapon aspects, and keepsakes. For a live play view, collapse these choices into a deliberate pause or reward state; do not leave a large panel over combat. The included specimen uses synthetic names and values.
