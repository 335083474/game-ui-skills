# In Other Waters scientific instrument reference

Use this experimental package to create original interfaces informed by the exploration, navigation, scanning, and cataloging premise of *In Other Waters* (2020). It is not an official art pack or an exact recreation of the game UI.

Read [visual rules](references/visual-rules.md), [screen patterns](references/screen-patterns.md), and [sources and limits](references/sources.md). Reuse the original contour artwork and editable specimens in [assets/components/index.html](assets/components/index.html). The specimen page is a component plate, not one of the requested full screens.

Build the visual hierarchy from a readable instrument field: cream ground, precise cyan mapping lines, compact coordinate labels, and a small warm-yellow heading or sample signal. Use contour paths, radial sweep marks, location pins, and line-linked observations as the main graphic language. Keep the surrounding chrome quiet so the mapped surface carries the sense of exploration.

All names, species marks, coordinates, routes, scan results, and field notes in this package are invented. The contour and specimen drawings are original vector art. The broad science-fiction and ocean-navigation direction comes from the official listing; the specific radial instrument, palette, and line treatment are editorial interpretations. No official screenshots, game art, or extracted assets are included.

See [USAGE.md](USAGE.md) for a prompt for the three approved screen types.
