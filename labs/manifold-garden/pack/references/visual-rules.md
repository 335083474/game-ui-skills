# Visual rules — impossible architecture direction

The plan selects Manifold Garden for abstract geometry and sparse persistent UI. The official store listing describes impossible architecture and geometry that repeats infinitely. The components below interpret that scene-first premise with original SVG architecture; they are not canonical screen captures.

## Scene language

- Let connected square arches, repeated portals, perspective seams, and a restrained plant motif carry the composition.
- Use deep green-black, pale stone, muted leaf, and one amber signal. Separate planes by geometry and light, not thick card borders.
- Keep perspective aligned and repeated forms spatially connected. Avoid random polygons as decoration.
- Keep interface words short so attention returns to the environment.

## Interface grammar

- **pause:** place a compact readable list at an edge. Freeze the scene and explicitly gate camera input while the list is active.
- **chapter-gates:** use a narrow, temporary label only during a deliberate transition. Leave architecture visible around it.
- **spatial-hint:** show one small geometric glyph plus one verb at an edge; hide it after the player acts or the hint expires.
- Do not surround the first-person view with persistent panels or turn spatial puzzles into a dashboard.

## Type and accessibility

- Use local sans-serif for brief state labels and local monospace for short technical hints.
- Keep contrast high using a quiet backing field; show keyboard focus as a clear edge or underline.
- Pair color with text or geometry, respect reduced-motion preferences, and reserve transitions for pause or orientation changes.

## 3D playfield protection

Keep the center and lower-middle movement area clear. At rest, show at most a small edge status or contextual prompt. In pause or pointer-driven menus, suspend camera look and restore it only after dismissal. On narrow screens, preserve the scene crop and shorten labels instead of expanding overlays.
