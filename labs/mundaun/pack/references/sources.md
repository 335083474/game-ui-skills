## Official source

- [Official Steam product page for Mundaun](https://store.steampowered.com/app/720350/)

The official listing describes Mundaun as a hand-penciled horror tale set in an Alpine valley and names a unique hand-penciled aesthetic.

## Approved editorial scope

The approved implementation-plan row selects the pencil family and experimental-art-direction suitability for the 2021 PC original. It proposes pencil hatching across spaces and objects, and approves satchel, journal, and trail-map. The row states that this is an editorial database gap and makes no measured demand claim. This plan evidence is recorded here so the package remains self-contained.
