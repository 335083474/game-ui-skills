# Visual rules

## Surface and composition

Treat the screen as a flat, warm electronic instrument. Use a pale beige field (#c9c3ae) with a nearly invisible square grid, a faint edge vignette only at the outermost boundary, and thin hairline dividers. Keep large empty areas around the primary list or grid. The low-chroma surface is essential: the design is not a bright white app and not a black tactical HUD.

Use a horizontal category rail above the content, with small uppercase or title-case labels and a short black bar under the active category. Separate neighboring areas with thin vertical rules. Build content from rows, slots, and aligned text, not rounded cards. Keep the main content wide and shallow; leave a quiet margin around it.

## Type and selection

Use a neutral humanist sans at 14–18px for labels and values, with tabular numerals for capacity, quantities, and levels. Main headings may use a slightly heavier weight, but avoid oversized display lettering. Keep labels, selected names, and help text in the same compact scale.

Selection uses two cues together: darker text and a 2–3px black rule at the row edge or beneath the active tab. Do not flood the row with a color fill. Focus remains visible with a thin black outline and small offset. Disabled options use reduced contrast plus an explicit reason.

## System geometry and ornament

Use a 16–24px baseline grid. A 1px hairline separates rows; every few rows may use a double-line terminal mark. The local score-rule SVG gives a reusable abstract musical measure motif: sparse parallel lines and a closing bar, never a literal staff of notes. Keep motifs to corners or section ends, not behind text.

Use a small square grid at 20–28px spacing with 3–5% opacity. Do not distort type or apply CRT curvature as a default. The grid should disappear at normal reading distance. Reserve red-orange for damage or warnings; reserve pale green for a positive state. Never encode the only state difference through tint.

## Chip and inventory grammar

Chip sockets are empty outlined squares in a compact matrix. Installed chips show a short name, category glyph, and cost; capacity appears as a numeric total plus a restrained thin meter. The meter is secondary to the number. Never turn chip slots into colorful rarity cards. Item rows use small monochrome pictograms, quantity aligned to the far edge, and a one-line description below the active row.

## Motion, accessibility, and responsive layout

Use short, linear transitions only for changing rows or opening a detail pane. Respect reduced motion. Keep keyboard focus and pointer hover visually equivalent. On narrow screens, preserve the horizontal category rail as a scrollable row and stack detail text beneath its list; never shrink the numeric labels to fit. The demo's web tabs are implementation scaffolding, not a claim about a specific controller mapping.
