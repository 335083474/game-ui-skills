# Visual rules

## Ledger grammar
- Use only near-black and warm paper white for primary status and content.
- Keep observed facts, inferred explanations, and unresolved identities distinct by words, symbols, and row labels.
- Align names, locations, and status into a stable reading grid. Dense data may wrap but must not reorder meaning.
- Dither and hatch are optional texture for large quiet areas only. Keep letters crisp and high contrast.
- Use one clear block cursor or inversion for focus. Keyboard focus cannot depend on pixel animation.
- Detail surfaces may be compact; the crew index, current record, and back path stay discoverable.

## Palette, type, and shape
Use tokens.json. Monospace is a system stack and no font file is included. Prefer square rules, thin double dividers, sparse separators, and rigid columns. Avoid gradients and colored glows.

## Avoid
generic shop, loot, reward, or inventory surfaces; multicolor glow; smooth gradients; ambiguity between suspected and confirmed identities. Don't add generic inventory/shop/reward layouts.
