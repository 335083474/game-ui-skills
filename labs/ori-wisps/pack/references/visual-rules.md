# Visual rules

## Atmosphere and contrast
- Work in deep layered blue-green forest values; reserve cyan and amber for small luminous signals.
- Use thin organic rules, branching silhouettes, soft painted transitions, and open negative space.
- Keep text on steady, dark or light opaque surfaces. Do not lay body copy across painted foliage.
- Use glow sparingly, never as the only focus indicator. Pair selected/current/locked states with labels and distinct node shapes.
- Keep card edges thin and material-led. Avoid generic glowing card grids and saturated neon gradients.
- Animation should be slow and restrained. Keep interaction feedback immediate and provide reduced-motion behavior.

## Screen-specific rule
Ability nodes can form a loose constellation but must have a readable linear focus order and explicit prerequisite/cost. Shards need slot, benefit, and pending/applied state. The map must have shape and text for current, discovered, and unknown locations; the route cannot depend on color alone.
