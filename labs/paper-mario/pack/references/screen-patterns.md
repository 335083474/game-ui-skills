# Screen patterns

## badges

Show each badge as a clear paper token with an original emblem, name, one-line effect, and capacity cost. Place equipped and available badges in a perforated folio or short vertical list. Give the current selection an ink outline and textual state. Show remaining capacity with separated pips. Keep equip as a distinct confirmation action.

## items

Present a small set of loose item slips with original icon art, quantity, short purpose, and an optional use target. A selected slip may lift slightly, but keep its edge and label readable. Separate preview from use and show a brief local status after the action.

## battle-orders

Use a shallow stage, a prominent command ribbon, and an explicit target. Keep audience feedback as a secondary meter at the stage edge. Use one strong selected command at a time. On mobile, stack the stage cue above commands and preserve large tap targets.
