# Sources and asset boundaries

- [Nintendo Australia: Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door](https://www.nintendo.com/au/games/nintendo-switch/paper-mario-the-thousand-year-door/) is the official product link selected by the approved plan. Opened on 2026-10-16 to confirm the title and Nintendo Switch product context. It does not establish this package's component layout or exact visual rules.
- Fixed study target: 2024 Nintendo Switch remake direction.
- The art direction and reusable component grammar here are original interpretation. No source-game screen was traced.

## Original package files

- references/images/reference-board.svg and assets/art/ui-atlas.svg are original SVG illustrations. They contain no extracted screenshots, logos, sprites, textures, or character designs.
- assets/components/index.html and assets/components/theme.css are editable local HTML/CSS specimens. Controls change local presentation state only.
- No external image, font, script, or runtime network request is required.

## Limits

The official page establishes product context, not the interface details in this package. This is an original art-direction study, not an official UI specification or engine export. Example names, counts, costs, and states are invented. Search volume and ranking are unknown.
