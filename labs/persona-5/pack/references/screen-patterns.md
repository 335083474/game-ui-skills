# Screen patterns

These are composition contracts for new work, not finished game screenshots. The component specimen uses synthetic content and original vector art.

## `party` — Crew Lineup

A lineup makes role and readiness legible before personality copy; distinguish each member with a symbol and name.

**Sample state:** A compact lineup makes role and readiness visible before the next risky move.

## `equipment` — Loadout

Equipment rows keep slot, object, and one stat delta together so a player can compare quickly.

**Sample state:** Show the worn piece, the slot it fills, and its single clearest effect.

## `requests` — Requests

A request is a short evidence card with one objective, a deadline cue, and a text-bearing status.

**Sample state:** A request is a short piece of evidence with one objective and an honest status.

## Shared interaction

Use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, labeled selected and unavailable states, and a responsive layout. Keep motion restrained and respect reduced-motion preferences.
