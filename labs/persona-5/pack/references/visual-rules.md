# Visual rules

## Shape and material

Compose with hard red, black, and warm white paper. Use sharp cuts, slanted labels, heavy black contours, and clipped halftone corners. Let a few edges break their panel, but keep descriptions on flat high-contrast paper. Avoid soft gradients and rounded dashboard cards.

## Type and emphasis

Use a local condensed sans for short, loud headings and an upright sans for descriptions. Tilt a label or shape, not a whole paragraph. Keep red for the selected member or decisive action; repeat the selection in text and with a clear focus contour.

## Collage patterns

The party screen is a lineup of distinct silhouettes and role labels. Equipment pairs one item with one stat change. Requests read like torn dossiers: a short objective, a deadline cue, and a visible state. Make the three patterns related through cuts and type, not identical cards.

## Adaptation

Use [screen-patterns.md](screen-patterns.md) for party, equipment, and request studies. All characters, names, values, and request text in the specimen are invented. The package is a 2022 PC Persona 5 Royal visual-direction experiment, not official UI.
