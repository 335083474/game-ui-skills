# Visual rules

## Paper, ink, and hierarchy

Use a warm paper ground (#eadcb8) with subtle fiber speckle and uneven ink rules. The parchment should read as the work surface, not a full-bleed beige rectangle behind floating cards. Use an inset double border or lightly ragged paper edges; avoid polished metal framing and glossy rounded corners.

Headings use a restrained old-style serif, body text uses a readable serif or humanist fallback, and handwritten accents appear only for short recipe marks. Keep body copy 14–17px with tabular numerals for prices and quantities. A hand-drawn label may be slanted by a few degrees, but do not rotate body text or make every label script.

## Map and potion grammar

The Alchemy Map is the focal diagram. Draw a dark ink route that meanders through the map field; mark ingredient nodes with small circular or stamped symbols and effect nodes with named colored circles. The player traces a path by selecting an available node; a separate danger cloud or obstacle boundary shows route risk. Put the active route and destination in the map itself, not in an unrelated progress bar.

Potion bottles are squat or bulb-shaped silhouettes with corks and thick dark outlines. Bottle glass carries the only saturated color family: ruby, cobalt, amber, or sage. Ingredient art stays botanical and matte. Keep the provided atlas images large enough to read, but do not tile the entire atlas as decoration.

## Recipe and trade surfaces

Recipes read like an indexed ledger: ruled rows, a tiny bottle specimen, recipe name, effect, and bookmark or brew action. Trade reads as a counter transaction with the customer's request at top, selected bottle and offer in the middle, and a final price/accept control below. Prices align in a stable right column. Include inventory counts beside ingredients and disable additions when stock reaches zero.

Use a small wax-seal or tied-scroll motif as a state stamp. Do not use the wood plank frames, pixel crops, energy bars, or high-saturation shop tiles associated with other cozy games.

## Responsive and interaction rules

Use a desktop split with a broad map field and a narrow ingredient rail. Below 760px stack the ingredient rail under the route map and keep the current potion visible. Make route nodes at least 40px hit targets without visually oversized circles. Keyboard focus draws an ink outline; hover only darkens the contour. Respect reduced motion: no continuous floating bubbles. State changes may briefly pulse the destination ring.

All example customer names, ingredients, values, and brew effects are invented. Do not infer exact recipes or prices from this reference.
