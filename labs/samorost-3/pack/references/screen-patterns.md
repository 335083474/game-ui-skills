# Approved screen patterns

These are patterns for the approved player tasks. Keep the specimens modular; they are not three complete screens.

## object-combination

Present a small number of objects with clear material and shape differences. Let the player select two; show the pair connected as a reversible preview. “Combine” is a distinct action. After the attempt, name the result or explain that the combination did not work. Preserve unknown outcomes as unknown until game state provides the answer.

## pictorial-hints

Use an ordered sequence of at most four symbols or scene fragments. The sequence should communicate direction, cause, or position without paragraphs. A reveal control makes the clue state explicit. Provide an accessible text equivalent and a way to hide or revisit the hint without resetting other puzzle state.

## planet-chapters

Show a small orbit or connected set of worlds with current, visited, and unavailable states. Use each world's silhouette plus a short label; do not imply that unvisited means locked. A selected world is a route preview until “Set route” is committed. On small screens, use a scrollable orbit with keyboard-reachable stops.
