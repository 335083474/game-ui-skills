# Visual rules

## Street-print surface

Build visual energy from large flat ink silhouettes, torn paper stickers, tape strips and halftone bursts. Use a deep near-black contour and a paper-white or warm grey base. Rotate cyan, electric lime and coral-orange between teams and actions; use one dominant ink color per module. Never let several saturated colors compete for the same label.

Use condensed bold sans for short headings, numbers, and player names. Slant display labels by 4–8 degrees or set them in a skewed sticker shape. Keep normal reading text upright. Use two type sizes for hierarchy, not a giant editorial headline. Prices, kill counts, and territory percentages remain tabular and right-aligned.

## Sticker anatomy

A sticker is an irregular polygon with a small shadow or offset second paper layer. A paint swipe may break the card boundary but should not reduce text contrast. Halftone is a spot texture clipped to the outer corner of a shape. Tape appears as a narrow diagonal band, never as a page border.

Gear modules use one recognisable silhouette or symbol, a short item label, and one or two compact attributes. Weapon, sub-weapon, and special-weapon are separate stacked tags. Do not imitate an official logo or weapon icon. Keep generated sneakers and goggles as generic kit decoration only.

## Catalog and results

Catalog rows look pasted onto a wall: uneven sheet widths, small price stickers, and a thick chosen-item contour. A selected row may rotate slightly and sit a few pixels forward. Result modules use a bold territory percentage or score plus name-bearing team labels, and one clear placement ribbon. Use shape, text, and pattern as well as color to distinguish teams. Keep the center numeric result unobstructed.

The result layout is a game summary, not an app dashboard. Use an irregular two-team split, a turf strip, and compact medal/streak decals. Avoid equal rounded cards, admin tables, and static charts.

## Interaction, motion, and mobile

Use a small bounce or 2–3 degree paper tilt only on selection and purchase confirmation. No continuous jitter. Respect reduced motion. All selections, currency, and team outcomes need text labels and keyboard focus; focus uses a visible white/black double contour. At narrow widths, stack sticker rows and put the main result number first. Do not scale dense text as a poster.

Suggested screen state and interactions are in [screen-patterns.md](screen-patterns.md); names, costs, and match stats are synthetic.
