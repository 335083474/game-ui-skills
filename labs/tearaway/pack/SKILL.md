# Tearaway Unfolded paper UI reference

Use this reference when the requested interface should feel cut, scored, folded, and assembled from paper. This is an independent visual study, not an official asset pack or exact recreation.

Read [visual-rules.md](references/visual-rules.md), [tokens.json](references/tokens.json), and the requested pattern in [screen-patterns.md](references/screen-patterns.md). Use [reference-plate.png](references/images/reference-plate.png) for actual paper fibers, folds, cut edges, and cast shadows. The art is original and contains no source-game character.

The approved directions are collection-scrapbook, paper-outfit, and folded-world-map. The [component specimens](assets/components/index.html) isolate reusable paper objects; they are not finished screens. Build hierarchy from paper planes and real creases, not stitched felt or a generic flat card grid.

Keep selection reversible until a labeled pin, unfold, or apply action commits it. Preserve visible focus, keyboard operation, contrast, narrow-screen reflow, and reduced-motion behavior. The specimen interactions change only local page state.

The edition boundary and generated-art provenance are in [sources.md](references/sources.md) and [asset-map.json](references/asset-map.json). No official characters, source-game artwork, remote fonts, engine scenes, or verified search-demand data are included.
