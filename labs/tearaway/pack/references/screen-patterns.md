# Approved screen patterns

Use these patterns as task-specific grammar. The specimen board shows reusable parts, not three finished pages.

## collection-scrapbook

Lay out collected paper pieces like cutouts tucked under a fold or into a pocket. Preserve a visual difference between new, already collected, and unavailable objects. Selecting an item shows a reversible preview; “Pin to book” is the explicit commit. Keep each cutout's silhouette and label distinct, and let the page scroll rather than shrink pieces on mobile.

## paper-outfit

Keep the paper character or costume silhouette visible. Show a small row of cut-paper options with shape and label, then preview the selected piece on the figure. “Fold onto outfit” or an equivalent named action applies it. Preserve before/after feedback and avoid stacking multiple folds where they obscure the character.

## folded-world-map

Use a continuous route strip with 2-4 readable panels that open from the map's fold. Each stop has one icon, one title, and a completion state. Selecting a stop previews the route; “Unfold route” commits the destination. On smaller viewports, let the paper strip scroll horizontally with keyboard-accessible previous and next controls.
