# Screen patterns

## inventory

Use a square grid of original item symbols with a clear active slot, item name, and short assignment hint. Keep the red focus frame distinct from the gold panel edge. At phone width, reduce columns only as needed while keeping touch targets at least 44px.

## dungeon-map

Represent the dungeon as small square rooms connected by explored passages. Label the current room and distinguish unexplored rooms with a separate shape or text cue. Keep the map compact and console-like; do not turn it into a modern full-screen map.

## item-shop

Show a short merchant list with original item symbols, readable names, and sample prices. State that values are demo data. A selected offer is only a preview until the separate confirm action; a local specimen never implies that a purchase was saved.
