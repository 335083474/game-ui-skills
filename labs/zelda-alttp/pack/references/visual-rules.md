# Visual rules

## Keep the 1991 SNES grammar

Use a dark dungeon field, compact rectangular windows, square equipment slots, double gold edging, and a small red selection frame. Red hearts read as a simple life row. Keep the palette limited and pixel-like geometry crisp.

This package targets the 1991 Super Nintendo direction. Avoid modern Zelda conventions such as a large translucent minimap, radial item wheel, cinematic objective panel, or floating 3D markers. The reference illustration is original, not a source-game screenshot.

## Inventory, rooms, and shop

Give every slot a visible name or selection label. Use map cells for rooms, with current, explored, and unknown states distinguished by labels or symbols as well as color. A shop row shows item, price, and preview/confirm boundary. Treat sample prices, names, and room data as invented.

## Type and interaction

Use short system-serif headings and system sans or monospace for controls and counts. Keep borders square, icon silhouettes simple, and long explanatory copy out of the compact game surface.

Use semantic controls, stable keyboard order, visible focus, and at least 44px touch targets. At narrow widths, preserve square slots and a readable heart row. Respect reduced motion and do not imply that a demo action changed a real save.
